Upstream removed fasciae and selected connective structures, decimated and compressed the geometry, and supplied named anatomical parts. Its [third-party licence notice](https://github.com/tiralonghipol/free-muscles/blob/605dd1ca351421ad874a03f673a2a3f915ab254e/LICENSE) identifies this GLB as CC BY-SA 4.0. The upstream application's MIT licence does not replace the artwork licence.

**Plate adaptation:** Tianyi Mao / Strength Atlas. Decoded and lit the model; projected the anterior and posterior orthographic views; used the same depth-resolved named meshes to derive the visible hit contours. The anterior projection includes a teaching window through the overlying abdominal layers to expose rectus abdominis. The groups aggregate multiple named mesh portions (for example the four quadriceps muscles). These plates are teaching illustrations, not measurements. For reuse, retain attribution, identify changes, and share adapted artwork under the same licence.
